Film Work

More than ten years working across film and television production, with a focus on the technical side of filmmaking — from DIT and on-set media workflows to Video Assist and VTR.

On Set
My background in film and television is rooted in technical production. I work primarily with Video Assist, VTR and DIT, alongside occasional work in the camera department. The work sits at the intersection of cameras, image, data, technology and the people who need those systems to work reliably on set.
