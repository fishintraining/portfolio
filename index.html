About
I’m a civil engineer with a background in film and a growing obsession with how people interact with technology.

I have a Master’s in Human-Computer Interaction from KTH, where I’ve explored that intersection through UX and UI design, research, analysis, prototyping and user testing. I like taking an idea from something vague and hard to explain into something you can actually interact with — and then seeing what happens when someone else tries to use it.

I’m curious about the relationship between people and technology, and about the difference between how something is supposed to work and how it actually works in the real world.

Background
I’ve spent more than ten years working in film and television, starting in post-production before moving into technical roles on set. I’ve worked across editing, colour, audio, media workflows, DIT and Video Assist, on everything from TV and commercials to film productions.

Film taught me to be comfortable when things are complex, fast-moving and rarely go exactly as planned. I’ve learned to stay calm under pressure, solve problems as they happen, communicate with very different kinds of people, and quickly understand what needs to happen next.

It also made me curious about the relationship between people and technology — and about the difference between how something is supposed to work and how it actually works in the real world.
